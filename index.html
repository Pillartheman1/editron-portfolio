I treated it like a junior editor. It cut a version, I wrote notes, and each correction became a written rule or an automated check. Over nine days in October 2026 it edited real client work for a jewelry store, a tech startup and my own media company. Build's lessons from my Resolve timeline went back into Editron's rulebook.
